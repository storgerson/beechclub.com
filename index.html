Why Fly With Us

Everything you need, none of the hassle

Club ownership means lower costs and less overhead than renting or owning solo — without giving up the freedom to fly whenever you want.

Two Turbo-Normalized Bonanzas

Both club Bonanzas are hangared at Georgetown Airport (KGTU) and maintained on a strict schedule by our A&P/IA.

Predictable, Fair Costs

Shared ownership means lower hourly rates and dues than renting from an FBO, with transparent, member-set pricing.

A Real Community

Monthly safety meetings, weekend fly-outs, and a hangar full of members who love talking airplanes as much as flying them.
