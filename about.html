About Beechclub

A small club, built around two great airplanes

We're a member-owned flying club based at Georgetown Airport (KGTU), flying two turbo-normalized Beechcraft Bonanzas — N33TN and N17826.

Our Story

Why Beechclub exists

Beechclub was formed by pilots who wanted the freedom of aircraft ownership without going it alone. By sharing two well-equipped, turbo-normalized Bonanzas among a small group of members, everyone gets access to serious cross-country capability — high-altitude performance, speed, and range — at a fraction of the cost of owning outright.

Both aircraft are hangared together at Georgetown Airport (KGTU), so members fly out of one home base and get to know the airplanes, the hangar, and each other well.

What We Value

Our mission

Three things guide how the club operates day to day.

Safety First

Standardized checkouts, recurrent training, and open reporting of squawks — no aircraft flies until it's right.

Ownership Without the Overhead

Members get airplane access without the hassle of solo ownership — maintenance, hangar, and insurance are handled by the club.

A Tight-Knit Group

We keep membership small enough that everyone knows the airplanes — and each other — well.
