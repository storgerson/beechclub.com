How It Works

Joining the club

  1. Reach out

    Send us a message through the contact page or come to a monthly meeting to introduce yourself.

  2. Meet the members & see the aircraft

    Tour the hangar at Georgetown Airport (KGTU) and meet current members flying N33TN and N17826.

  3. Checkout flight

    Complete a checkout with a club-approved instructor or safety officer in each aircraft you plan to fly.

  4. Sign up & start flying

    Pay dues, get scheduling access, and start booking N33TN or N17826 online.
